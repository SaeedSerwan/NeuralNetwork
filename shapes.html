03Training: guess, compare, correct

A network does not start out knowing shapes. Its weights begin random, so its first answers are guesses. For each training example the network predicts, the prediction is compared with the real answer, the error is sent back through the network (backpropagation), and every weight is adjusted a little. After enough examples, it predicts correctly most of the time.

04Where neural networks are used

images

Face recognition

Phones unlock and photos are tagged by recognising faces. Usually a CNN.

sequences

Forecasting

Rainfall, electricity demand, stock prices: predicting the next values from past ones.

generation

Music and art

Networks learn patterns in music or images and compose new pieces.

language

Chatbots and translation

Transformers read and write text: ChatGPT, Claude, machine translation.

medicine

Medical images

Finding tumours in scans or eye diseases in retina photos.

security

Fraud detection

Flagging unusual card payments: see data imbalance.
